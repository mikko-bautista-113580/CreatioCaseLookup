Reassigned

Owner changed at least once. Handoffs through a blank owner count once.

Does reassignment create a new row? Yes.

What to plan for in the time calculations

  • One row per change, not per statusA new row starts when status, owner, group, priority or service item changes. Consecutive rows often share a status, so group by status before summing.
  • Handoffs pass through a blank ownerOwner A → (no owner) → Owner B is common. Count reassignments between named owners, or each handoff counts twice.
  • New repeats at the startMost cases open with two or three New rows seconds apart while the service item and deadline are set. Treat them as one New period.
  • Status can move backwardsSome cases go In progress → New, for example when sent back to the queue. Don't assume a forward-only order.
  • The Closed row never endsIts EndDate is 0001-01-01 rather than blank, and its duration columns are 0. It's shown as "open" here; leave it out of time totals.
  • No substatus in the historyCaseLifecycle has no substatus column, so client-blocked and internally-blocked waiting can't be separated from these rows.
  • Calendar time onlyDurations are End − Start in calendar time. There's no business-hours adjustment in the data.
