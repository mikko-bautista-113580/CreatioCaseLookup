Connection settings

These are saved to the app's .env file. Cookies take effect on the next search — no restart needed. Base URL, allowlist and row cap changes need an app restart.

Session cookies (SSO)

Click below to open a browser at the Creatio login page. Once you finish signing in there (including any MFA prompt), the session cookies are captured automatically — no copy/paste needed.

Enter cookies manually instead

Fallback if no browser can be launched: get these from your browser after logging in to Creatio — DevTools (F12) → Application → Cookies → your Creatio URL. Copy the values. Leave a field blank to keep the current value.

Team wiki

Case-scoped analyses pull matching pages from the Custom Team wiki in Azure DevOps, using your Azure CLI login (az login) — no token is stored. Change the wiki with ADO_WIKI_ORG, ADO_WIKI_PROJECT and ADO_WIKI_ID in .env, or turn it off with CREATIO_WIKI_ENABLED=false.

Team skills

Fix plans are built from the team's skills — /Skills/<name>/SKILL.md in the Custom-Team repo — read through the same Azure CLI login. Change the source with ADO_SKILLS_REPO, ADO_SKILLS_PATH and ADO_SKILLS_BRANCH in .env, or turn it off with CREATIO_SKILLS_ENABLED=false.
